about the studio

TBHN is an independent game development studio where we create projects that seem awesome (and sometimes weird)

We build in C#, Lua, Python, and JavaScript across Unity, Roblox, and mobile. Our work spans multiplayer state synchronization, client-side prediction, spatial partitioning, runtime optimization, asset pipelines, and automated releases.

Alongside the games, we share the process. Devlogs, showcases, and community spaces have reached more than 1.8 million views and connect thousands of players and developers.
